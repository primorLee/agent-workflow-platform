# Code of conduct

AWP is a technical collaboration space. Participants are expected to be direct
about evidence while remaining respectful toward people.

## Expected behavior

- Critique claims, designs, tests, and code with concrete reasoning.
- Distinguish a reproducible defect from a preference or unsupported guess.
- Respect privacy and remove sensitive information from public reports.
- Welcome contributors regardless of background, identity, experience, or
  communication style.
- Accept correction and update a claim when the evidence changes.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing,
deliberate exposure of secrets, and sustained personal attacks are not
acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close participation, or
ban an account when necessary to protect the project and its participants.

For ordinary technical disagreement, use an issue or pull request. For a
confidential conduct or security concern, use the repository's
[private security-advisory channel](https://github.com/primorLee/agent-workflow-platform/security/advisories/new).
Reports will be reviewed on their evidence and handled with the minimum
disclosure needed to investigate.
